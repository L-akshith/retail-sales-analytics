# Business Recommendations

## Retail Sales & Customer Analytics — Actionable Recommendations

> All recommendations below are directly connected to findings from the data analysis. No recommendation is made without supporting evidence.

---

## Recommendation 1: Implement a 20% Discount Ceiling Policy

**Connected Finding:** Discounts above 20% generate negative average profit. The company lost ~$135,376 from orders discounted above 20%.

**Recommendation:**
- Establish a company-wide maximum discount of 20% on standard orders.
- Discounts above 20% should require manager-level approval and a documented business justification (e.g., strategic clearance, customer acquisition).
- Monitor discount adherence through a monthly dashboard metric.

**Expected Impact:** Recovery of a significant portion of the $135K in losses currently caused by excessive discounting.

---

## Recommendation 2: Conduct a Strategic Review of Tables and Bookcases

**Connected Finding:** Tables (-8.6% margin) and Bookcases (-3.8% margin) are consistently loss-making sub-categories despite generating substantial revenue.

**Recommendation:**
- Investigate whether losses stem from COGS, shipping costs, heavy discounting, or return rates.
- Evaluate supplier alternatives or renegotiate pricing.
- Consider reducing SKU count in these sub-categories to focus on profitable items.
- If structural losses persist, evaluate whether to exit low-margin SKUs.

**Expected Impact:** Eliminating or reducing losses in Tables and Bookcases could improve Furniture category margin from 2.5% toward 10%+.

---

## Recommendation 3: Prioritize Technology and Copier Product Lines

**Connected Finding:** Technology leads in profit margin (17.4%), and Copiers are the highest-margin sub-category (~37%).

**Recommendation:**
- Increase marketing investment for Technology products, particularly Copiers and Phones.
- Explore cross-selling and upselling opportunities within Technology.
- Allocate premium placement (physical or digital) to high-margin products.
- Develop bundle offerings that pair high-margin Technology products with complementary Office Supplies.

**Expected Impact:** Shifting product mix toward higher-margin categories improves overall profitability without requiring revenue growth.

---

## Recommendation 4: Launch State-Level Profitability Audits for Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, and Illinois

**Connected Finding:** 10 states are unprofitable. Texas alone generates $170K in revenue but loses $25,729. These are not small markets — they are high-revenue, negative-profit territories.

**Recommendation:**
- Audit the top 4 loss-making states (Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Illinois) individually.
- Analyze product mix, discount patterns, shipping costs, and customer segments in each state.
- Compare pricing strategies between profitable states (e.g., California, New York) and unprofitable ones.
- Implement state-specific pricing or shipping adjustments as needed.

**Expected Impact:** If these 4 states could be brought to breakeven, the company would recover ~$70K in annual profit.

---

## Recommendation 5: Implement Customer-Level Profitability Tracking

**Connected Finding:** The highest-revenue customer (Sean Miller, $25K revenue) is unprofitable (-$1,981 loss). Revenue-only tracking misidentifies customer value.

**Recommendation:**
- Integrate profit margin as a standard metric in the CRM system alongside revenue.
- Create a customer scorecard that includes: Revenue, Profit, Margin, Order Frequency, and Average Order Value.
- Flag customers with revenue > $5,000 but margin < 5% for account review.
- Train sales teams to balance revenue targets with profitability awareness.

**Expected Impact:** Prevents revenue-destructive discounting for key accounts and ensures that high-touch customer management is directed toward truly profitable relationships.

---

## Recommendation 6: Optimize Q1 Performance Through Targeted Promotions

**Connected Finding:** Strong seasonality — Q4 (November/December) generates the highest revenue, while Q1 consistently underperforms.

**Recommendation:**
- Design Q1-specific promotions targeting Office Supplies (high-margin, consumable products) to smooth seasonal revenue dips.
- Align inventory procurement with seasonal patterns — increase Q3 purchasing for Q4 demand.
- Avoid margin-eroding clearance sales in Q1; instead, focus on value-added bundles.
- Consider a loyalty/rewards program that incentivizes Q1 purchases.

**Expected Impact:** Smoothing Q1 revenue improves cash flow predictability and warehouse utilization.

---

## Recommendation 7: Benchmark and Improve Central Region Performance

**Connected Finding:** The Central region's profit margin (7.9%) is nearly half of the West region's (14.9%), despite significant revenue ($501K).

**Recommendation:**
- Benchmark Central region operations (pricing, discount rates, product mix, shipping logistics) against the West region.
- Identify and address the specific factors driving margin compression in the Central region.
- Consider region-specific pricing adjustments or product catalog optimization.
- Set a 12-month target to improve Central's margin from 7.9% to at least 10%.

**Expected Impact:** A 2-percentage-point margin improvement in Central would generate ~$10K additional annual profit with no revenue growth required.

## Consulting Perspective

### Business Problem
The retail company has achieved consistent revenue growth over 4 years but faces significant profitability challenges in specific product sub-categories, geographic markets, and customer accounts. Aggressive discounting and lack of margin-focused management have eroded profits.

### Analytical Approach
1. **Data Collection & Cleaning:** Acquired and cleaned 9,994 transactional records spanning 2015–2018.
2. **Descriptive Analytics:** Computed KPIs (revenue, profit, margin, AOV) at company, category, product, customer, and regional levels.
3. **Diagnostic Analytics:** Investigated root causes of profitability gaps through discount analysis, sub-category profitability, and state-level P&L.
4. **Exploratory Analysis:** Created 12 business-focused visualizations to identify patterns, trends, and outliers.

### Key Findings
1. Technology is the most profitable category (17.4% margin); Furniture is the least (2.5%).
2. Discounts above 20% are consistently unprofitable — ~$135K in losses.
3. 10 states operate at a net loss, including high-revenue markets like Texas.
4. Revenue concentration among top customers is moderate (top 20% = 48%).
5. Strong Q4 seasonality requires proactive planning.

### Business Implications
- The company is growing revenue but risking long-term profitability through undisciplined discounting and neglect of product-level margin management.
- Geographic expansion should be paired with margin targets, not just revenue targets.
- Customer management should shift from revenue-centric to profit-centric.

### Recommended Actions
1. Cap discounts at 20% (recovery potential: ~$135K)
2. Audit and restructure Furniture sub-categories (Tables, Bookcases)
3. Invest in Technology/Copier product lines
4. Conduct state-level profitability audits for top 4 loss-making states
5. Implement customer-level profitability tracking in CRM
6. Optimize Q1 promotions and seasonal inventory planning
7. Benchmark Central region against West region operations
